# WhyDrop seeded dataset: ground truth (for evals only, never shown to the agent)
Generated by `scripts/generate_data.py` (seed 42), Jun-Sep 2026. Net revenue: Aug about $13.4k, Sep about $8.9k (a 34% drop).

Planted causes, roughly in order of impact:
1. Broken Meta "fall_launch" campaign (from 2026-09-08): spend continues (~$2.4k in Sep), clicks steady, but landing pages 404 (`web_traffic.json` landing_404s), so paid_social orders fall from ~196 in Aug to ~28 in Sep.
2. Price rise on P001 "Notion Freelance OS", $19 to $29 on 2026-09-01 (`pricing_changes.json`): about 55% of would-be buyers walk away, so P001 revenue falls despite the higher price.
3. Refund spike on P003 "Python Scraper Pack" after v2.0 release on 2026-09-15 (`release_log.json`): "file corrupted" refunds (26 in Sep, 0 in Aug) and download support tickets ("Zip won't open").
Red herrings: email traffic is flat; there is a mild ~3% seasonal dip across all sources; country mix is unchanged.

Sources (to federate through Zetaris): orders.csv, products.csv, marketing_spend.csv, web_traffic.json, pricing_changes.json, release_log.json, support.db (SQLite: refunds, support_tickets).
