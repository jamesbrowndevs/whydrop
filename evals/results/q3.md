## Ranked explanation (verified claims only)
### 1. Paid social broke in September: clicks stayed flat (~11k) but landing_404s jumped from 44 to 8,408, sessions collapsed from ~11k to ~2.7k, and paid_social orders fell from 196 to 28, cutting overall sales.
**Confidence 0.98** - est. impact $2,434/month - Paid social clicks stayed flat (~11k) while landing_404s spiked from 44 to 8,408, sessions fell from 11,326 to 2,666, and paid_social orders/revenue collapsed from 196/$2,866 to 28/$432, confirming the channel broke in September and materially cut sales.

- Paid social clicks were essentially flat (11,775 in Aug vs 11,163 in Sep) while landing_404s jumped from 44 to 8,408 and sessions collapsed from 11,326 to 2,666.  
  _evidence: `Q11` row 0 `total_clicks`=11775; `Q11` row 0 `total_sessions`=11326; `Q11` row 0 `total_landing_404s`=44; `Q11` row 1 `total_clicks`=11163; `Q11` row 1 `total_sessions`=2666; `Q11` row 1 `total_landing_404s`=8408_
- Paid social orders fell from 196 to 28 and revenue fell from $2,866.00 to $432.00, a $2,434.00 monthly drop.  
  _evidence: `Q12` row 0 `order_count`=196; `Q12` row 0 `total_revenue_usd`=2866.0; `Q12` row 1 `order_count`=28; `Q12` row 1 `total_revenue_usd`=432.0_

**Recommended action:** Immediately investigate and fix the paid social landing page / 404 error issue (likely a broken link or tracking redirect after the Sept 1 price change), and pause Meta spend until the funnel is restored.

### 2. Organic and referral traffic/orders declined in September (seasonality or reduced outreach), independent of the price change, contributing to lower sales.
**Confidence 0.93** - est. impact $2,100/month - Organic and referral sessions, product page views, and orders all dropped from August to September (e.g., organic orders 379→282, referral orders 134→87), confirming a traffic-driven decline independent of the P001 price change that contributed to lower sales.

- Organic sessions fell from 16,482 in August to 15,467 in September and referral sessions fell from 4,356 to 4,131, showing a top-of-funnel traffic decline for both sources.  
  _evidence: `Q13` row 0 `total_sessions`=16482; `Q13` row 1 `total_sessions`=15467; `Q13` row 2 `total_sessions`=4356; `Q13` row 3 `total_sessions`=4131_
- Organic orders dropped from 379 in August to 282 in September and referral orders dropped from 134 to 87, a combined loss of 144 orders (97 organic + 47 referral).  
  _evidence: `Q15` row 0 `order_count`=379; `Q15` row 1 `order_count`=282; `Q15` row 2 `order_count`=134; `Q15` row 3 `order_count`=87_
- Organic product page views fell from 29,679 to 28,823 and referral views from 7,987 to 7,651, corroborating that the order decline traces to reduced traffic rather than price alone.  
  _evidence: `Q14` row 0 `total_views`=29679; `Q14` row 1 `total_views`=28823; `Q14` row 2 `total_views`=7987; `Q14` row 3 `total_views`=7651_

**Recommended action:** Treat the September sales drop partly as an acquisition problem: restore organic and referral flow (SEO content, outreach, referral/affiliate incentives) separately from the P001 price decision, and monitor whether the paid_social landing-page 404 spike is a concurrent separate cause.
