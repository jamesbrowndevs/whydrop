## Rejected hypotheses (tested, evidence says no)
- **Meta paid_social traffic hit broken landing pages in September (landing_404s jumped to ~8,408), sending unqualified visitors who bought by mistake or felt the offer was not as described, increasing refunds from the paid_social channel.** - Paid_social 404s did spike to 8,408 in September, but paid_social refunds fell from 6 refunds / $64 in August to 1 refund / $12 in September, with bought-by-mistake (1→0) and not-as-described (4→0) refunds dropping to zero, so the claimed paid_social refund increase did not happen.  
  _evidence: `Q15` row 0 `total_404s`=44.0; `Q15` row 0 `total_sessions`=11326.0; `Q15` row 1 `total_404s`=8408.0; `Q15` row 1 `total_sessions`=2666.0_

  _evidence: `Q8` row 0 `old_price_usd`=19.0; `Q8` row 0 `new_price_usd`=29.0; `Q8` row 0 `date`=2026-09-01_
