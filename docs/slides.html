📉 WhyDrop

The agent that can explain why sales dropped, and proves every claim.

GenAI Works Open Agent Hackathon 2026 · Track 2 · Nathan Melia · github.com/jamesbrowndevs/whydrop

Problem & users

  • Small online sellers (Gumroad, Shopify, Etsy) watch revenue fall with no analyst to ask.
  • Dashboards show what dropped, not why.
  • The clues are scattered: order exports, ad spend, traffic, refunds DB, price and release logs.
  • Generic chatbots guess, and an unverified "why" leads to wrong fixes.
WhyDrop

Solution

Ask in plain English: "Why did revenue drop in September?"

  • Ranked causes with confidence and estimated $ impact
  • Every claim cites exact cells (query · row · column · value)
  • Rejected hypotheses shown with their evidence
  • A recommended action per cause; Markdown report export
WhyDrop

How it works

① Signal scan on Zetaris→② Plan hypotheses + recall memory→③ SQL per hypothesis
④ Analyse → cited claims→⑤ Verify every claim→⑥ Ranked report→⑦ Remember (H-MEM)

Hypotheses are tested in parallel; the UI streams every step, query and model call live.

WhyDrop

Trust: no claim without evidence

  1. The cited query is re-run on Zetaris, and the result hash must reproduce
  2. Each cited cell must exist and match
  3. Every number in the text must come from cited cells (or a simple delta, ratio or %)
  4. An independent Nemotron verifier lane must agree

Failing claims are struck out and never reach the answer.
